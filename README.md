# Product Funnel & Retention Optimization

A product analytics project focused on understanding **user funnel drop-offs, retention behavior, churn patterns, and subscription cancellation** to identify opportunities for improving user engagement and lifetime value.

##  Product Objective

> **Where are users dropping off, why are they churning, and which product interventions can improve retention?**

The project analyzes the user journey across key lifecycle stages and translates behavioral patterns into actionable product strategies.

---

##  Product Funnel

```text
User Acquisition
       ↓
     Sign-up
       ↓
    Onboarding
       ↓
   Activation
       ↓
   Engagement
       ↓
 Subscription
       ↓
    Retention
       ↓
 Repeat Usage
```

The analysis focuses on identifying the stages where users experience the largest drop-offs and determining opportunities for product intervention.

---

## 📊 Key Analyses

### 1. Funnel Analysis

Analyzed conversion and drop-off rates across the user journey to identify high-friction stages.

**Key metrics:**

* User conversion rate
* Stage-wise drop-off
* Activation rate
* Subscription conversion
* Cancellation rate

### 2. Cohort Retention Analysis

Created user cohorts based on signup/activation periods and tracked retention across:

* **D1**
* **D7**
* **D30**
* **D90**

This helps distinguish short-term engagement from sustained product adoption.

### 3. Churn Analysis

Identified behavioral patterns associated with subscription cancellation by analyzing:

* Engagement frequency
* Usage patterns
* Subscription behavior
* Funnel progression
* Retention cohorts

High-risk behavioral segments were then mapped to potential retention interventions.

---

##  Product Opportunity

The analysis was translated into an **AI-driven onboarding guide** designed to provide personalized recommendations based on user behavior.

```text
User Behavior
      ↓
Behavioral Profile
      ↓
Risk / Engagement Signals
      ↓
Personalized Guidance
      ↓
Improved Activation
      ↓
Higher Retention
```

The proposed intervention targets a **20% reduction in subscription cancellations** through personalized onboarding and proactive engagement.

> **Note:** The 20% figure represents the project's target/expected impact, not a measured production result.
